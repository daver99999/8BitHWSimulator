# Timex Sinclair TS2068

The board is the TS2068 PC board **335-850001-03**, built from the Timex Sinclair 2068
Technical Manual: the schematic (335-870500) and the component layout (Appendix D). The
netlist was transcribed by hand (`tools/ts2068-netlist-src/`, rebuilt into
`src/ts2068_board_data.c` by `tools/ts2068_netlist2c.py`); the ICs, the dock connector J4,
the keyboard connector J9 and the SCLD sit where the layout drawing puts them, the small
parts are in a row below the board.

Written by Claude (Anthropic's AI), directed by David Green (daver99999), from the Timex
documentation and the CC-19 service data (timexsinclair.com).

## ROMs

They are Timex's and not included. Put the two images in `roms/ts2068/` (also looked for in
`roms/` and the current folder; they are recognised by size, not name):

- the 16 KB home ROM (U16, `ts2068-0.rom`)
- the 8 KB EXROM (U20, `ts2068-1.rom`)

## What is simulated

The Z80A at 3.528 MHz, the two ROMs, the 4416 DRAMs (display RAM U6/U7, upper RAM
U12/U13 and U17/U18), the 74LS157 address multiplexers, the 74LS244/245 buffers, the
AY-3-8912 (U14), the keyboard matrix with its diodes, the joystick diodes and the dock
connector. The SCLD (U3) has no datasheet: it is a model written from the manual's description
of what it does (video timing and fetch, the contended clock, ports F4/FE/FF, the AY
strobes, /INT, the ROM and EXROM selects). The display RAM contention rules, the
position and length of /INT and the display fetch timing are **assumed**, not measured
from a real board.

The SCLD's pins were beeped out on a real 03D board by David Green and are marked as checked (his word; the individual results
were not written down here). One measurement is recorded in the netlist: DRAM A0 (U12 pin 14) is not connected to U11 pin 12.

With the ROMs in place the machine boots to the "© 1982 Sinclair Research Ltd / ©1983
Timex Computer Corp" screen, and BASIC runs from the keyboard.

## Not done

- **Your board's patch board.** Some 03D boards carry an undocumented daughterboard
  ("Bank Switching / ULA Logic Patch Board") on the SCLD. It is not in the manual and is not
  modelled; the simulator follows the manual's rev 03 schematic.
- The RF modulator is a separate part and is not simulated.
- No tape, no file loading, no sound output, no joystick input from the host; the
  fault atlas and the diagnosis tools do not cover the TS2068.
- **The dock connector J4** is the 64 contact cartridge dock: 32 positions, a B (solder) side and an A (component) side, with the pin list from the Technical Manual (given by David Green; both ends of both sides are ground). The simulator numbers the pins 1B=1, 1A=2, 2B=3, 2A=4 ... 32B=63, 32A=64. Not connected in the netlist: +15V, the tape EAR input, R G B and VIDEO, SOUND, DZIN/DZOUT, BUSISO, IOA5, pin 9B ("0" in the list) and the Slot and Unused positions (they are analog, video or not simulated). The dock used to be a 36 pin connector read from a crop of the schematic; its odd pins were the least certain part.
