# Timex Sinclair 1000 (ZX81), board Issue 3

`--machine ts1000`, or Timex > TS1000 in the controls.

**Netlist:** transcribed from the schematic "Sinclair ZX81, DRW No. SRC 049, re-drawn by Ron Reuter"
(www.mainbyte.com/ts1000), which has the USA-only parts (R30-R32, D9) and the resistor packs that the Issue 3 board has
as R35-R42 and R43-R47. Parts: IC1 ULA, IC2 ROM (2364, 8K), IC3 Z80A, IC4 RAM (2K: a 6116 type in the 4118 footprint,
A10 on pin 19 through link L2), TR1 (/WAIT), TR2 (clock inverter), R7-R14 470 ohm data resistors, R18-R26 1K address
resistors, R2/R28 680 ohm chip-select resistors, R15 + C5 the reset RC, R16/R17 the /BUSRQ and /WAIT pull-ups, D1-D8 the
keyboard diodes, KB1/KB2, the edge connector J1.

**Checked on a real Issue 3 board:** R35-R42 on the data lines, R43-R47 and KB1 (pins 1-5 = KBD0-4), KB2 (pin n on diode Dn) and the diodes' address
lines (D1-D8 on A11 A10 A12 A9 A13 A8 A14 A15). **Issue 1 board:** RP1 (pin 1 the common, pins 2-9 on D1 D0 D7 D2 D6 D5 D3 D4), RP3 (pins 6-2 on KBD0-4) and KB2 (pin 1 on D8, in order down to D1), and D1-D8 on A11 A10 A12 A9 A13 A8 A14 A15, were all checked on a real board; the parts are placed from photos of it (good to a few mm, the names of the small resistors round the jacks are guesses).

**Not checked:** the modulator pins stay as drawn from the schematic (video, USA3, 0V, +5V): the owner's board has had its modulator replaced by a composite
video mod, so there is nothing to measure. The jacks and the RAM chip type are also still inferred. They are marked INFERRED in `tools/ts1000-netlist-src/sheet01.py`.

**Placement** is from a top photo of an Issue 3 ZX81 board (same board and silkscreen as the Timex; drawn as the photo shows it: edge
connector at the top right, jacks at the left, keyboard connectors at the bottom right), good to a mm or two. Every part is placed from
the photo, the USA parts (R31, R32, D9) at their unfitted silkscreen. Where the silkscreen was not readable the name inside a group is
a guess (the small resistors round the ULA and at the edge connector, R35-R38); R39-R42 follow the data lines measured on a real board.
The photo board has a 7805 and the 2114 RAM pair, which are not modeled (IC4 is drawn between the two RAMs).

- ROM: put the 8K ZX81 ROM (`.rom`/`.bin`, 8192 bytes) in `roms/ts1000`. It is not shipped.
- The ULA is a functional model (clock, sync counters, NMI, keyboard/tape port, video fetch); the oscillator always runs.
- The Z80 /RESET is driven low for 50 ms at power on instead of simulating the R15/C5 RC.
- Not modeled: the 7805, the modulator and the video levels, the tape circuits, the RAM pack, loading programs.
- Keyboard: RUBOUT is the same as DELETE (Backspace on the PC).
