# Timex Sinclair 1500

`--machine ts1500`, or Timex > TS1500 in the controls.

The TS1500 is not a TS1000 with 16K of RAM. The ZX81's 40 pin ULA is replaced by **U5, a custom 68 pin gate array**
("COMPUTER LOGIC" on the schematic) that also runs the memory: eight 4116 DRAMs (U8-U15, 16K x 1) with /RAS, /CAS, /WE and
the multiplexed address RA0-RA7, and a 74LS245 data transceiver (U6) and 74LS244 address buffer (U3) switched by U5's /DEN
and /AEN. The ROM (U2, 8K) is the TS1500's own.

## How good is this netlist?

It is transcribed from a **1024 x 653 image** of the schematic. What could be read is solid: the chips and refs, the buses, the
signal name on every chip pin, the DRAM wiring, U3 and U6, the keyboard (40 keys, D1-D8, KBD0-KBD4) and the edge connector.

**The pin numbers of U5 could not be read** (only 59 TAPE IN and 54 CH SEL OUTPUT are legible). They are counted along the
schematic's symbol (left side from the top 59..43, bottom from the left 42..22, right side from the bottom 21..4, top from the
right 3, 2, 1, 68..60), so the *names* on U5 are right and the *numbers* are a guess that has to be checked on a real board.
Everything inferred is marked INFERRED in `tools/ts1500-netlist-src/sheet01.py`. The board view draws U5 as a standard
PLCC-68 (pin 1 at the middle of the top side), so its drawn pin positions do not match the real chip either.

The placement is a rough layout, not read from a board.

## Continuity test for U5

Power off, the 68 pin chip's pins are the PLCC's: pin 1 in the middle of the top side (the chip's notch/dot corner), then
counterclockwise. For every row, buzz the **real** U5 pin that goes to the part on the right, and write the real pin number down.
If it agrees, the row is right.

| Signal (U5 pin in this netlist) | Buzz it to |
|---|---|
| GND (57), +5V (58) | the ground and +5V rails (there may be more supply pins: find them all) |
| MREQ (1), WR (2), M1 (3) | Z80 U1 pins 19, 22, 27 |
| RD (65), HALT (66), IORQ (67) | Z80 U1 pins 21, 18, 20 |
| NMI (68), WAIT (64) | Z80 U1 pins 17, 24 |
| CPUCLK (6) | Z80 U1 pin 6 (CLK) |
| CLK (5), CLKN (4) | the edge connector's clock contact / the option pad |
| OSCIN (7), OSCOUT (8) | the two sides of crystal X1 |
| RESET (9), RFSH (10) | Z80 U1 pins 26, 28 |
| D0-D7 (11-18) | Z80 data pins 14, 15, 12, 8, 7, 9, 10, 13 (the CPU side of U6) |
| /DEN (19) | U6 (74LS245) pin 19 |
| /AEN (20) | U3 (74LS244) pins 1 and 19 |
| /ROMCS (21) | ROM U2 pin 20 |
| A0'-A7' (22-29) | ROM U2 pins 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 (the ROM side of U3) |
| A8' (30) | ROM U2 pin 23 |
| A9-A15 (31-37) | Z80 U1 pins 39, 40, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 |
| KBD0-KBD4 (38-42) | keyboard connector KB1 |
| RA7-RA0 (43-50) | the DRAM address pins (RA0-RA6 on every DRAM's A0-A6; RA7 probably unused) |
| /WE (51), /CAS (52), /RAS (53) | DRAM pins 3, 15, 4 |
| CH SEL OUTPUT (54) | modulator channel select |
| TAPE IN (59) | EAR jack circuit |
| TV TAPE (60), CONTROL (61) | the video / tape circuit |
| RAMRM (62) | edge connector (the TS1000's /RAMCS contact), RAMCS (63) | edge connector side |

Also worth checking: the order of D1-D8 on the address lines A8-A15, the pin numbers of KB2 (read as 6 3 2 1 4 5 7 8 for
A8..A15), which pin of U6 DIR is on (here /RD), and where the RESET pull-up and the keyboard pull-ups are (here inside U5).

## Model

- **U5** is the ZX81 ULA's behaviour (`src/ula81.c`, now taking a pin table) plus the DRAM timing: /RAS when /MREQ and A14
  and not a refresh, /CAS three half periods after the access (/RD or /WR) starts, RA0-RA6 the row (A0-A6) and then the
  column (A7-A13). The WAIT line (the TS1000 makes it with transistor TR1) is made inside U5.
- **Assumed, not known:** the character bitmap path in a video fetch (read from the ROM's outputs while U6 is switched off),
  DRAM refresh (not simulated: the model keeps its data), the +12V and -5V of the DRAMs (their pins are unchecked).
- 16K RAM at $4000-$7FFF. The ROM finds RAMTOP = $8000.
- The ROM boots slowly (about 4 seconds of emulated time to the K cursor): it clears and expands the display file.
- ROM: put the 8K TS1500 ROM (`.rom`, `.bin` or `.u2`, 8192 bytes) in `roms/ts1500`. It is not shipped.
- Not modeled: the power section (the 5V regulator, the -5V/+12V converter), the modulator and the video levels, the
  tape circuits, the expansion pack, the TV channel select keys.
- Headless check: boots to the K cursor and runs `PRINT 1` (`0/0` report at the bottom).
